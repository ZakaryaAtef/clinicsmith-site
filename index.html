ClinicSmith

Clinic management: patients, visits, prescriptions and appointments.

ClinicSmith runs a clinic from Windows: reception and each doctor's queue, patients' files with every visit, prescriptions, lab and radiology requests, appointments with online booking, and the clinic's accounts. It is made for private clinics and medical centres, with one doctor or many and one branch or several, and works in Arabic, English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Turkish.

Get it from Microsoft Store

What it does

  • Reception and each doctor's daily queue.
  • Patient files: visits, diagnoses (ICD-10), vital signs, allergies and chronic conditions, images and documents.
  • Prescriptions, lab and radiology requests and medical reports, printed on the clinic's letterhead.
  • Appointments by doctor and branch, online booking for patients, and reminders by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Accounts: fees, debts, expenses, insurance and contracted companies, employees and salaries.
  • Dental charts, diet plans and clinic supplies.
  • Staff accounts with roles and permissions, and several branches.

How it works

ClinicSmith is the Windows app of an online service, so it needs an internet connection and an account. Open the app, choose Create a new clinic on the sign-in screen, then add your staff in Users & permissions. Every PC and laptop of the clinic signs in to the same clinic, so reception and the doctors see the same patients and appointments.
